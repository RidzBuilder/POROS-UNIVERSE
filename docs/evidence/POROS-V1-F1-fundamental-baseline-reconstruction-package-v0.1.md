The following are reconstructed as **candidate invariants**.

## I-01 Evidence integrity
No architectural or conformance claim may be upgraded without evidence appropriate to the claim.

## I-02 Epistemic preservation
A status must not silently upgrade from source/claim/candidate to validated/canonical.

## I-03 Authority separation
Capability availability does not imply permission or authority.

## I-04 Semantic separation
Distinct semantic stages and objects must not be collapsed merely for implementation convenience.

## I-05 Provider separation
Concrete providers/tools must not become the semantic definition of a capability.

## I-06 Adapter boundary
Provider/platform changes should be isolated behind explicit capability/adapter contracts where the architecture requires replaceability.

## I-07 Runtime proof
Agentic/conformance claims require runtime evidence, not diagrams, prompts, or documentation alone.

## I-08 Reproducibility
A validation result must identify enough inputs, environment, execution, output, and criteria to be independently reconstructed.

## I-09 Traceability
Material decisions, actions, results, evidence, and provenance must remain traceable.

## I-10 Controlled evolution
Implementation change must not silently redefine the fundamental semantic core.

## I-11 Failure visibility
Failure, missing evidence, and unresolved gaps must remain visible; they cannot be converted into success by omission.

## I-12 Scope integrity
A domain-specific reference case or AOS research model must not automatically become a universal POROS rule.

## I-13 Human/authorized decision gate
Canonical architecture requires explicit authorized approval.

## I-14 No premature implementation
Implementation does not begin from a draft baseline unless a separate authorized decision explicitly permits it.
