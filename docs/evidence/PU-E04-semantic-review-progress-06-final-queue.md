## 3. Cross-item observations and unresolved reconciliation

### 3.1 Repeated candidate themes
Across reviewed items 0001–0131, the source corpus repeatedly discusses capability/tool separation, implementation/provider independence, boundary separation, traceability, evidence production, controlled evolution, specification-to-instance separation, and readiness gates. Repetition is not independent corroboration when statements derive from the same author/source chain, and does not confer canonical authority.

### 3.2 Conflicting or differently scoped models
The corpus includes multiple diagrams and flows: capability/layer models, traceability/evidence lifecycle, multiverse ecosystem, and agent specification-to-readiness pipeline. They have different scopes and stage granularity. They must not be collapsed into one universal POROS architecture or workflow without a source-to-claim crosswalk, conflict/supersession analysis, explicit decision owner, and recorded approval.

### 3.3 Authority and validation
- Source statements labeled “strong candidate,” “candidate,” “validation reference,” “baseline unchanged,” “complete,” or “Library archived” remain assertions from the bundled records until corroborated by the authoritative underlying record or live system.
- The queue does not include independent reviewer attestations, primary approval signatures, complete source-chat records, all referenced PDFs, or Library item/version identifiers sufficient to close authority and completeness.
- No independent test results can be inferred from references to documented stress-test tracks or acceptance criteria; the underlying test evidence must be examined.

### 3.4 Canonical disposition
No item in this batch is promoted to POROS canonical. This batch completes semantic queue review only. Candidate themes may enter a separate controlled synthesis/reconciliation phase only after the applicable source recovery and approval gates are satisfied.
